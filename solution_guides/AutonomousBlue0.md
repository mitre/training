1. Click the `+ New Operation` button to open the `Start New Operation` menu.
1. Give the operation this exact name: `Blue Autonomous`.
1. Select the `Incident responder` adversary from the `Adversary` dropdown.
1. Select `response` from the `Fact source` menu.
1. Select `batch` from the `Planner` menu.
1. Select `Run autonomously` in the `Autonomous` radio group.
